The Best Thing I Have Read Recently

Recently I had read a poem that I had really felt and understood.
It was called “having “having a coke with you” with you” by mark leidner it was about someone talking with their friend,
their friend asked them if they knew a poem called “having a coke with you” they replied that they vaguely remembered it
but they didn't really at all. So their friend recited it in its entirety, as they walked down the street from one end of
the city to the other the whole time their friend kept going. As it continued they weren't sure when it was going to end
thinking “was that the last line of the poem” after every sentence, as the poem grew longer and longer it was becoming
harder and harder to believe that their friend had been reciting it from memory. Until near the end of the poem they
stopped thinking about how long it was and tuned back into what they were actually saying, which they had only kind of
been listening to. As it finished they believed the fact that their friend could recite it was the sole reason they had
done so, but it had not been to solicit appreciation for the fact that they could but to ask a question. They originally
had thought the poem was about “how liberating banal being in love with someone is” but recently they started to think it
was more about the “Idiocracy of caring at all when you could spend all that energy caring about someone you loved instead”.
But the question they had been wondering is where they stood on their interpretation and what they had thought about their
change of opinion on the poem now that they have heard it again. They were stunned, but not only by the fact that their
friend had recited such a good long poem so easily, but that it was just to ask such a simple question and not to show off,
but just to get my opinion and that was the moment they realized they wanted to be with them forever.
This is a short summary of the poem, it was almost like a short story but something about it was so touching to me, the mutual understanding, the care and love for life their friend has that they appreciate and care for but cant always bring forth the same energy, the want to listen yet you just get bored as a story you had heard before and forgotten is being told again. You can feel the love through the words even before the profession at the end, the annoyed gleefulness as they in their head ask again and again is this the last line of the poem. Not because they don't want to hear their friend speak but because they thought the fact they recited it was pointless, only realizing just at the end they really wanted to listen, immediately being thrown into another quandary not sure how to answer but realizing they were in love. It feels like the journey of falling in love in such a short amount of lines and I really appreciated it.
